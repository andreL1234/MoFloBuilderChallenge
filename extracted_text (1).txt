SMB Knowledge Activity Instructions
Introduction
Congratulations on advancing to the next stage of the interview process. We’re excited to see
how you will approach building. This assignment will test your ability to understand system
architecture, follow pre-existing practices, and create meaningful functionality that aligns with
the mission of MoFlo.

About MoFlo Cloud
MoFlo Cloud is a comprehensive business automation platform that provides AI-powered tools
for various business functions. All of our customers are small businesses that are very much
non-technical and are not great with complex interfaces or learning new technologies. We aim
to do as much for them as possible rather than making them do everything within our platform.
Applications are the core products inside MoFlo Cloud. Each app has a specific function (e.g.,
social posts, emails, blogs), and a specific connection point (to external platforms to push
content, integrate accounts, track results, etc.)
Our platform currently includes (with some examples of function + connection):
●​ MoSocial
○​ Function: Social media content generation (captions, hashtags, images)
○​ Connection: X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Youtube, TikTok
●​ MoMail
○​ Function: Email content generation (outbound emails, newsletters, etc.)
○​ Connection: Mail Chimp, Constant Contact, etc.
●​ MoBlogs
○​ Function: Blog content generation (blogs, case studies, articles, etc.)

○​ Connection: Wordpress, Wix, Framer,, etc.
All of our apps rely on a company knowledge base that we build for each business when they
sign up for MoFlo Cloud. This knowledge base is the foundation that allows every application to
generate tailored, on-brand content. The quality of the knowledge base directly determines the
quality of everything MoFlo produces.

Your Assignment: The Knowledge Builder
You will build your own version of our Knowledge, a web-based tool that scrapes a company's
website and transforms the extracted data into a structured, comprehensive knowledge base.
This is one of the most critical pieces of the MoFlo platform. When a new customer signs up, we
need to understand their business deeply and quickly. Your challenge is to build the system
that makes that happen.

What You're Building
A functional web application where a user can:
1.​ Input a company website URL
2.​ Scrape that website to extract meaningful business information
3.​ Display the scraped results in a structured, reviewable format
4.​ Allow the user to review, edit, and enhance the extracted knowledge
5.​ Save the final knowledge base as a structured JSON output

●​ Industry outlook (if available)

●​ Art style description

●​ Social media links (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X, etc.)

Offerings

Think Bigger
The categories above are our current baseline. A major part of this challenge is demonstrating
that you can think beyond the baseline.
Consider: if MoSocial needs to write social posts, MoMail needs to write emails, and MoBlogs
needs to write articles, what additional information would make those outputs better? What
else could you extract from a website that would be valuable across all of our applications?
Some directions to consider (these are prompts, not requirements):
●​ Competitor information or market positioning signals
●​ Content themes or topics the company frequently discusses
●​ Testimonials, reviews, or social proof
●​ Certifications, awards, or trust signals
●​ FAQ content or common customer questions
●​ Calls-to-action patterns and conversion language
●​ Unique selling propositions or differentiators
●​ Seasonal or time-sensitive messaging patterns
●​ Legal or compliance language (privacy policies, disclaimers)
●​ Media mentions or press coverage
●​ Community involvement or values-driven messaging
The best submissions will demonstrate original thinking about what makes a knowledge
base truly useful for AI-powered content generation.

Required Features
Scrape & Build Page (/knowledge)
This is your main page. It should include:
●​ URL input where the user enters a company website
●​ Scrape trigger that initiates the website scraping process
●​ A loading/progress state while scraping is in progress
●​ Structured display of results organized by knowledge base categories
●​ The ability for the user to review and edit the extracted information before saving
●​ A save button that converts the final knowledge base into a JSON structure
●​ Form validation and error handling
Important notes on scraping:
●​ You should build a real scraper that fetches and parses website content. This is a core
part of the challenge.
●​ You do NOT need to call any LLM APIs. If you want to demonstrate how AI would
enhance the extracted data (e.g., generating a company pitch from raw content), use
placeholder/mock outputs and clearly label them.
●​ Focus on extracting as much structured information as possible from the raw HTML,
metadata, and page content.
View/Manage Page (/knowledge/view)
This page shows knowledge bases that have been created. It should include:
●​ Display of saved knowledge base entries (from your JSON output)
●​ Multiple view modes (e.g., card view, table view, detailed view) that work well with the
content structure

●​ Filtering and/or search functionality
●​ Edit and delete functionality
●​ Any additional utilities that make sense for managing knowledge base records

Prompting & AI Thinking (Evaluated)
Even though you are not required to integrate a live LLM, we are evaluating your prompting
skills and AI thinking. Specifically:
1.​ Prompt Design: If your application were to pass scraped content to an LLM for
enrichment, what would those prompts look like? Include at least 2-3 example prompts
in your codebase or documentation that demonstrate how you would instruct an AI to
transform raw scraped data into structured knowledge base fields. These can be in a
dedicated /prompts directory, in your README, or as constants in your code.
2.​ Data Quality Thinking: How would you handle incomplete data? What fallback
strategies would you use when a website doesn't have certain information? Document
your approach.
3.​ Knowledge Enrichment Ideas: Beyond what's on the website itself, what other data
sources or enrichment strategies could improve a knowledge base? Include your ideas
in your documentation.
